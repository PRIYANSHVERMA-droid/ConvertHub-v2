# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

ConvertHub v2 is an offline-first desktop application designed with your privacy as a fundamental priority. All file processing and conversions happen entirely on your local machine; your files never leave your computer. There are no user accounts, no analytics tracking, no telemetries, and no advertisements.

## What We Collect

Nothing. ConvertHub v2 does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage statistics, conversion logs, or crash reports. Your activity within the application remains entirely private to your device.

## Auto-Updater

The app includes an auto-updater feature to ensure you have access to the latest improvements and security fixes. When checking for updates, ConvertHub v2 makes a secure HTTPS request to GitHub Releases (`https://github.com/PRIYANSHVERMA-droid/ConvertHub-v2/releases`). During this standard HTTP request, GitHub's servers may log network metadata such as your IP address. This interaction is governed strictly by [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement). If you prefer not to check for updates automatically, you can disable this feature in the app settings.

## Third-Party Engines

ConvertHub v2 bundles pre-compiled binary engines — FFmpeg, LibreOffice, and 7-Zip — to perform local conversions and operations. These engines run strictly on your local machine within your system environment. They do not initiate network connections, transmit telemetry, or send data externally. All output generated by these tools is saved directly to your local disk.

## Children's Privacy

ConvertHub v2 does not target children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected directly in this file within the repository, along with an updated date at the top of the page.

## Contact & Support

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/PRIYANSHVERMA-droid/ConvertHub-v2/issues).
